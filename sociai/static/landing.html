Research opportunity inquiry

My name is Maya and I am a second-year student who is very passionate about environmental research. I came across your lab and found your work fascinating. I would love the opportunity to contribute to your research in any capacity.

Please let me know if you have any openings. Thank you for your time and consideration.

Could be sent to any professor. Says nothing about her work, or Maya's.

After · the Sociai draft77 words
To
Prof. Lena Ortiz
Subject
Your marsh sediment paper, and a question about fieldwork

Hi Professor Ortiz,

I'm a second-year in environmental engineering. I read your 2025 paper on restored marshes1, and the finding that small tidal channels trapped more sediment than the plantings surprised me. My fieldwork last summer assumed the opposite.

You mentioned a follow-up field season2 in your department talk. I'd love to help: I've done core sampling and I'm comfortable in R3.

Would you have 15 minutes in the next two weeks to talk about it?

Thanks,
Maya

  1. 1From her lab page and the paper's abstract
  2. 2From a recorded department seminar
  3. 3From Maya's resume

FAQ

Questions

Does Sociai send emails for me?

No. Sociai only drafts. You copy the message and send it from your own email or account. It never connects to your inbox.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your profile, writing samples, resume and projects are visible only to you, and the database itself enforces that. We don't sell your data, and you can delete your account and everything in it at any time. Details in the Privacy Policy.

What's a credit?

One credit covers a research run on one person plus up to 3 drafts for them (a revision counts as a draft). New accounts start with 2 credits. If a research run doesn't finish, you get the credit back.
