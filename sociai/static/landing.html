How it works

From a name to a draft you'd actually send

  1. 01

    Tell it who and why

    Enter a name or a link and what you're asking for: a research position, a referral, a coffee chat, a first meeting.

  2. 02

    It does the research

    Sociai reads public pages about them, like lab sites, papers, talks, company pages and news. It shows what it found and where, and you fix anything that's off.

  3. 03

    It drafts in your voice

    Drafts follow your writing samples and background. Ask for changes, edit it, then copy it and send it from your own email.

Why it works

Why the drafts get replies

Every draft is shaped by real cold emails that got replies, and by what Sociai learned about the person you're writing to.

  • Specific, not flattering

    It mentions something real they did, so it can't be mistaken for a mass email.

  • Short, with one clear ask

    Easy to read on a phone and easy to say yes to.

  • In your voice

    Built from your own writing samples and background, so it reads like you wrote it.

  • You stay in control

    Nothing is sent for you. You review every line before it goes anywhere.

FAQ

Questions

Does Sociai send emails for me?

No. Sociai only drafts. You copy the message and send it yourself, from your own email or account. Sociai never connects to your inbox.

Can I cancel the monthly plan?

Yes, anytime, from Account and billing in the app. Cancelling stops future charges, and credits already in your account stay there.

What does a credit get me?

One credit covers a research run on one person plus up to 3 drafts for them, and a revision counts as a draft. If you need more drafts, re-running the research costs 1 credit and adds 3 more. If research doesn't finish, the credit is refunded.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your profile, writing samples, resume and projects are visible only to you, and the database itself enforces that. We don't sell your data or share it for advertising, and you can delete your account and everything in it at any time. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
